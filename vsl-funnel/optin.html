Is this training

For You?

This is for you if:

  • Your spouse has checked out, moved out or said they're done, and you're the one still fighting.
  • You've tried talking, counseling or giving more, and they still feel further away.
  • They won't go to therapy, or they say there's nothing left to work on.
  • You want to understand what you can change, even if they aren't ready to take part.

This is NOT for you if:

  • You're looking for a way to pressure or convince your spouse to stay.
  • You want them to change without changing anything in you.
  • You expect one conversation to fix everything.
  • There's abuse or coercion in your home. Please reach out to a local support service first.

About Sophie Orozco

Why did I make

This Free Training?

Chris and I went from a summer romance to four babies in about five years. Somewhere along the way, we became roommates. Then, the week after my sister died, he told me he wanted a divorce.

I tried everything. Counseling he wouldn't go to. Every book. Even my own therapist said there was nothing I could do. So I stopped chasing, and I started with me. It changed everything.

That became the True Self Method. I made this training so you don't have to figure it out alone at 2am the way I did. It walks you through the three shifts I now teach men and women in every kind of marriage crisis, and what you can start changing in your own marriage today.
